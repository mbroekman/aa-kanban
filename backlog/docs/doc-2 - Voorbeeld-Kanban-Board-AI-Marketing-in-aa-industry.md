# Example Kanban Board: AI Marketing in aa-industry

This document describes an example setup for a Kanban board in `aa-kanban` specifically tailored for implementing "AI Marketing" functionalities within the `aa-industry` module.

## 1. Board Configuration
- **Board Name:** AI Marketing - Industry Reforged
- **Description:** Tracking of all marketing campaigns, AI copywriting, and market analyses generated by the Industry Reforged AI module.
- **View Groups:** Members of the marketing and industry groups.
- **Write Groups:** Marketing Directors, AI Operators.

## 2. Lists (Columns)

We recommend using a phased marketing funnel approach for this board:

1. **Backlog / Ideas**
   *Raw ideas for AI marketing campaigns, suggested by players or generated by the AI.*
2. **AI Prompts & Generation**
   *Tasks where the AI is instructed to generate text, images, or market descriptions based on provided prompts.*
3. **Review & Editing**
   *Human verification of the AI-generated content. Quality control and fine-tuning prior to publication.*
4. **Ready for Publication**
   *Approved content that is ready to be posted in-game, on alliance channels, or Discord.*
5. **Published / Active**
   *Marketing campaigns that are currently running.*
6. **Completed (Done)**
   *Finished or expired campaigns. Evaluation of impact (ROI).*

## 3. Example Cards

Here are a few examples of cards (with their labels and assignments) that you can create on the board:

### Card 1: Generate Market Analysis for Jita Trade Hub
- **List:** AI Prompts & Generation
- **Description:** Have the AI generate a market analysis on current T2 ship prices in Jita to prepare a marketing push for our own production lines.
- **Labels:** `Analysis`, `AI-Task`

### Card 2: Copywriting for new T2 Drones campaign
- **List:** Review & Editing
- **Description:** AI has generated the advertisement copy for the new T2 Drones. The text needs to be reviewed for Eve Online-specific terminology and lore.
- **Labels:** `Copywriting`, `Review Required`

### Card 3: Setup Discord Webhook for AI Marketing Alerts
- **List:** Backlog / Ideas
- **Description:** Integration to automatically post successful AI-generated marketing messages to our Alliance Discord (via `aadiscordbot`).
- **Labels:** `Discord`, `Integration`, `Technical`

## 4. Best Practices for this board

- **Use Labels:** Create labels such as `Copywriting`, `Images`, `Data Analysis`, `Urgent`, and `Review Required` for quick filtering.
- **Connect Discord:** Utilize `aadiscordbot` to send notifications whenever a card is moved to "Ready for Publication".
- **Keep it tidy:** Archive or delete cards that have been in "Completed (Done)" for more than 2 weeks to keep the interface clean.
